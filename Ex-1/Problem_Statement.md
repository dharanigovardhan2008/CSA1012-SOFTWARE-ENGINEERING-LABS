# Experiment 01 – Create a Kanban Board to Visualize the Tasks

## Aim

To understand the fundamentals of Agile project management by creating and managing a Kanban Board for tracking task progress through different stages of a workflow.

---

## Problem Statement

Create a Kanban Board to visualize the workflow of a project.

The Kanban Board should include the following columns:

- To Do
- In Progress
- Done

Add at least five sample tasks to the board and simulate the workflow by moving the tasks across the columns as they are completed.

---

## Objectives

- Understand the concept of the Kanban methodology.
- Visualize the workflow using a Kanban Board.
- Track the progress of tasks through different stages.
- Simulate task movement from "To Do" to "In Progress" and finally to "Done".

---

## Requirements

- Create three columns:
  - To Do
  - In Progress
  - Done

- Add a minimum of five sample tasks.

- Move the tasks between columns to demonstrate the workflow.

---

## Expected Outcome

A functional Kanban Board showing tasks moving through different stages of completion, demonstrating an effective task management workflow.
